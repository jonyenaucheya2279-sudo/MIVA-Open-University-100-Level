Biography

Learning security from the ground up

I'm a first-year student on the Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity programme at MIVA Open University, Nigeria. I chose cybersecurity because nearly every part of daily life now runs on systems that need protecting, and I want to be one of the people who understands those systems well enough to defend them.

My first year covers the groundwork: problem solving and algorithms, web technologies, mathematics and physics, alongside general studies in communication and Nigerian culture. I finished my first semester with a GPA of 4.29.

Studying online has taught me to manage my own time, which is why this site includes the academic planner I use to track deadlines.
